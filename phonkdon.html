ABOUT THE ALBUM

Nobody gave us a seat. We built the stage.

Phonkdon begins with national identity and explores the underground culture that challenges and reinvents it: grime, pirate radio, jungle and drum & bass, garage nights, and the pressure of constantly having to keep up.

Made in England

“Identity”

What does being made in England really mean? Beyond flags, stereotypes and nostalgia lies a country of different backgrounds, ambitions and contradictions. A new generation proud of where it comes from, but determined to shape what comes next.

“Grime / pirate radio”

Nobody gave the underground a seat at the table, so it built its own stage. A lyrical celebration of grime, pirate radio, independent voices, sharp wordplay and the confidence to create without permission.

“Jungle / drum & bass”

When the bass drops, backgrounds become secondary. A high-energy jungle and drum & bass journey about freedom, connection and strangers sharing the same dancefloor.

Under pressure

“Sparse vocals / heavy bass”

The weight of modern life: rising costs, uncertain futures, social expectations and the feeling of constantly having to keep up. Sparse vocals, heavy bass and an atmosphere that lets the music speak.
