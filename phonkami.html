ABOUT THE ALBUM

Shadow. Red moon. Last train.

Phonkami’s first three tracks follow a single night in Japan: old discipline against modern pressure, a moon that turns the dark into a kingdom, and tired dreamers riding the last train home with hopes they can’t abandon.
