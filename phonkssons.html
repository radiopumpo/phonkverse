Sånt är livet!

“That’s Life!”

Work, rent, bills, expectations — same old circus, different day. PHONKSSONS turn the everyday frustrations of modern Swedish life into a sarcastic, bass-heavy anthem. Because sometimes all you can do is laugh, turn it up, and say: Sånt är livet!

02
02

Lagom? Aldrig!

“Moderate? Never!”

Sweden has a word for keeping everything balanced: lagom. But what if you were never meant to fit the mould? A rebellious celebration of individuality, freedom and refusing to live by somebody else’s expectations. A little too loud, a little too wild — exactly how they like it.

03
03

Samma frekvens

“Same Frequency”

Different lives, different struggles, different faces in the crowd — yet somehow, we can still feel the same thing. PHONKSSONS explore loneliness, connection and the invisible bond created by music. You don’t have to be alike to share a frequency.
