ABOUT THE ALBUM

A street-level voice. No permission needed.

Phonkazo’s debut looks at modern Spain — its people, struggles, humour and dreams. From impossible rent to political tribalism, it uses rhythm and wordplay to challenge the noise without surrendering its own voice.
