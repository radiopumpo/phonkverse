Такая жизнь

Takaya Zhizn — That’s Life

From Moscow’s restless streets to the frozen edges of Siberia, everyday Russian life is full of contradictions. Old habits meet a digital generation, history meets modern chaos, and somehow people keep moving. PHONKOV delivers a sharp, darkly humorous portrait of a country too complex to fit into a headline.

02
02

Не по приказу

Ne po Prikazu — Not by Orders

A powerful statement about patriotism, choice and the human cost of conflict. Leaders may count battalions, but ordinary people count the loved ones waiting at home. PHONKOV questions the idea that loyalty means blindly following orders — because loving your country doesn’t mean treating its people as expendable.

03

Разные корни

Raznye Korni — Different Roots

Russia is more than one city, one culture or one identity. From Dagestan and the Caucasus to Tatarstan, Siberia and beyond, countless traditions and communities share the same vast land. A celebration of cultural diversity, strength and brotherhood — different roots, one shared future.
